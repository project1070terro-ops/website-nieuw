Cycling is more than speed and distance. With 15/70, the collective goal comes first: giving children a better future. That is why we consciously choose Save the Children. Their vital work reaches those who need help the most: vulnerable children who cannot stand up for themselves.

Every donation is a vertical meter. Every vertical meter brings us closer to a world where every child gets chances, regardless of the circumstances they are born into.

Terro is not just cycling for himself, but for all children who deserve a helping hand towards a hopeful future.

Within the FORZA FORTUNA Financial Group team, we believe that together we can truly make a difference.

Join us and donate to show that the cycling community has the power to change lives. Together we stand up for the next generation.
