The really bad weather won't arrive until after noon, so I set off quickly. A nice route through the back roads, with some sand, forest and gravel paths. Fun, while it's still dry. At Schaarsbergen I enter De Hoge Veluwe Park. The advantage when lots of wind and rain are expected: I have the Veluwe almost to myself.

Downside: well, the wind and rain. I'll get my money's worth from the entrance fee and ride two laps. Beautiful. The special thing is that it just keeps going, you don't run into a residential area after 5 minutes. Wonderfully quiet. At Jachthuis St. Hubertus (from the quiz question: architect?) there are at least 3 people. Then the next loop, Deeler Veld, and there I am outside again. And it's starting to rain. On again via Radio Kootwijk.
