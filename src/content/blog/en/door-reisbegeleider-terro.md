**Dunes, forests and villages**

image: /images/blog/training-week-1-foto1.jpg

The bike rides we made were not too long, about 3 to 4 hours per day, and always over flat roads. An advantage was that many of the routes were signposted by local 'ANWB signposts', so everyone could cycle at their own pace.

The trip only lasts nine days, but still we got to see many different landscapes. In Lithuania we visited the Curonian Spit, where we enjoyed riding through the local dune landscape. And in Estonia we made a trip in Lahemaa NP. This is one of the last primeval forests of Europe.

The villages you cycle through along the way are generally still very traditional; many young people have moved to the city.

**Three countries, three cities**

During the trip we visited the three capitals of the Baltic states: Vilnius, Riga and Tallinn. Fortunately, we always had a hotel in the city centre, so we had plenty of time to get a first impression of these cities, which have a very interesting history and well-preserved city centres.

I was surprised how modern these cities have become in a short time. Restaurants, terraces and shops: all up to date.
